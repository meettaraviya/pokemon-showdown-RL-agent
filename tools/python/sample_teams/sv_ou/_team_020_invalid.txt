Description by Fusien:
The original rendition of this team had Iron Defense Leftovers Tera Fire Roar Zamazenta; this variation of the team makes it Choice Band Zamazenta for much greater power (especially against the otherwise extremely threatening Nasty Plot Pecharunt), at the the drawback of a cut in Zama's defensive utility and losing the ablity to phase w/ Roar. Adamant is chosen for maximum power as the presence of Lokix helps with underspeeding Darkrai. A Life Orb set can probably be ran if one wants to have a strong Zamazenta without the downside of having to predict with Band, though the damage difference is notable and Life Orb chips Zama's important Hp. Variations of this team are mainly regarding its ability to deal with select threats. Tera Ghost Alomomola allows the team to perform better into Substitute/Heavy Slam opposing Zamazenta. Tera Ice Alo + Tera Steel Torn can be run to try to be better protected against Specs/Sub Kyu teams, but this comes at the risk of being much weaker to the mentioned Zamazenta sets. On this current paste, Tera Ice Bulky Treads is given as a compromise. Other Tera alternatives for Treads are Ghost or Water, the former which prevents spin/helps against fighting moves, and the latter of which better covers Iron Moth. Teras can be adjusted accordingly with regard to what Teras Alo and Torn choose to use. Zamazenta can use Tera Steel over Dark for a very strong Heavy Slam against some targets (40-49% vs Bulky Pecharunt), including the notably threatening Sinistacha, but Tera Dark provides a stronger measure vs Pecharunt overall. Weezing can use a Pain Split set if reliance on Wish Passing is desired to be reduced, and can also use Toxic Spikes or Sludge Bomb if wanted. Lastly, Treads can potentially use Knock Off over Ice Spinner, and Amulet Coin can be used over lefties to prevent Parting Shot/Defog, though this increases reliance on Wish to maintain health.
